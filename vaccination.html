Common questions

Is the vaccine safe?

Mild tiredness for a day is common. Your vet will explain what to watch for.

Can I walk my puppy after the shot?

Keep puppies away from other dogs until the full course is done.

What if we miss a date?

Book as soon as you can. Your vet will tell you if the course needs to restart.

Do you issue travel certificates?

Yes, for rabies and travel health. Ask when you book.
